# Reference ads

Three ads the team likes for their animation style, studied frame by frame to extract **mechanisms**: timings, easing, layouts and sound levels. The videos belong to their owners and aren't in this repo; only our written analysis is. Ask the repo owner for the original files if you need to look at them.

| Folder | Ad | Format | Most useful for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ad-1381884446693481/` | Superside "No-Hype AI Report" (presenter) | vertical 9:16, 58 s | **Closest to our format.** Speaker-to-card curve, caption behaviour, kinetic type, breathing zooms, logos behind the speaker |
| `ad-1079204844669188/` | ElevenLabs voice agents (presenter) | 16:9, 36 s | 7-frame snap zoom, panel rise, chat bubbles growing in, music levels under the voice |
| `ad-1839128747462053/` | AI agents ad (no presenter) | square, 30 s | Object-carried transitions (occluder wipe, orb portal), grey-to-white word fill, sparse sound |

Each `notes.md` has the measured findings. The rules they produced are already in `.claude/skills/business-motion-film/references/talking-head.md`, so read the notes only when you want the detail behind a rule.
